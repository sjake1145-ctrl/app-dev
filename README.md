# My Favorite Series: VisionQuest

## About
**VisionQuest** is a Marvel Television series on **Disney+** and the final chapter of the trilogy that began with *WandaVision* and continued with *Agatha All Along*. It follows Vision and explores identity, consciousness, and what it means to be human.

## Main Cast
- **Paul Bettany** as Vision
- **James Spader** as Ultron
- **T'Nia Miller** as Jocasta
- **Todd Stashwick** as Paladin


## Why I Like It
1. The story continues from *WandaVision* and *Agatha All Along*.
2. It explores deep themes about AI and humanity.
3. It has a strong cast of characters from across Marvel.

> "But what is grief, if not love persevering?"
